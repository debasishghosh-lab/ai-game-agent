THE DARK CASTLE

The Dark Castle is an abandoned fortress surrounded by a dense forest.

LOCATIONS

Castle Entrance:
The main entrance of the castle. It is damaged and covered with vines.
From here, the player can travel to the Guardian's Chamber or the Ancient Library.

Guardian's Chamber:
A large stone chamber containing the Ancient Sword.
A Stone Guardian protects the sword.
The guardian normally sleeps unless someone attacks it or tries to steal the sword.

Ancient Library:
An old library containing hundreds of books.
A secret passage is hidden behind a large bookshelf.
The secret passage leads to the King's Vault.

King's Vault:
A hidden vault beneath the castle.
It contains the legendary Crown of Shadows.
The vault has a magical lock.
