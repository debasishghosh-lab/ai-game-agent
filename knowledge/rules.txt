GAME RULES

D20 DICE

The game uses a twenty-sided dice.

Every dice roll produces a number from 1 to 20.

STEALING

When stealing an important item:

1-9: Critical failure
10-14: Failure
15-19: Success
20: Critical success

ATTACKING

When attacking an enemy:

1-5: Critical failure
6-10: Miss
11-19: Hit
20: Critical hit

EXPLORATION

Players can explore locations to discover objects,
characters, hidden passages and events.

HUMAN OVERRIDE

The Game Master must request human approval before major consequences.

Examples:

- Player death
- Permanent loss of an important item
- Killing an important character
- Major changes to the game world

The human can approve the proposed event or override it.
