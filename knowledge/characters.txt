CHARACTERS

STONE GUARDIAN

The Stone Guardian protects the Ancient Sword.

HP: 80

The guardian is extremely strong but slow.

It attacks anyone who tries to steal the Ancient Sword
or attacks it directly.

ELARA

Elara is a mysterious mage who lives near the castle.

She knows about the secret passage in the Ancient Library.

Elara will help the player if they speak to her respectfully.

If the player threatens Elara, she refuses to help.

OLD KING

The Old King is dead.

His spirit sometimes appears inside the King's Vault.

The spirit knows the history of the Crown of Shadows.
